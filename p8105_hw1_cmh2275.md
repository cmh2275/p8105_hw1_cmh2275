## Create dataframe

``` r
hw1prob2_df = tibble(
  norm_samp = rnorm(10),
  norm_samp_pos = norm_samp > 0, 
  vec_char = letters[1:10], 
  vec_factor = factor(rep_len(c ("low", "med", "high"),10)), 
)
```

## Take mean of variables - attempt 1

- The mean of norm_samp is 0.3495203.
- The mean of norm_samp_pos is 0.8.
- The mean of vec_char is NA.
- The mean of vec_factor is NA.

The means of character variables are not able to be calculated.

## Convert variables

``` r
as.numeric(pull(hw1prob2_df, norm_samp_pos))
as.numeric(pull(hw1prob2_df, vec_char))
as.numeric(pull(hw1prob2_df, vec_factor))
```

## Take mean of variables - attempt 2

- The mean of norm_samp is 0.3495203.
- The mean of norm_samp_pos is 0.8.
- The mean of vec_char is NA.
- The mean of vec_factor is 2.

It is now able to calculate a mean for the converted character factor
because it now assigned a numeric code, rather than a character code.
The character vector uses letters, which are not converted and therefore
converted to NA.
